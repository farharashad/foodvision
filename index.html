LAYER 2 · SERVICE

Adjustable decision rules

R1 · Suspected bruising / rot / visual anomaly8%

Above the limit, the item goes to REVIEW and sounds the alarm once. If a later camera frame crosses the limit before the item is removed, the existing record updates to REVIEW. Limit changes apply to the next item. At or below the threshold, a measured item automatically gets PASS and stays silent. Uncertain images still show a note. Reports without a measurement go to REVIEW silently. Thresholds are demonstration values, not validated produce-specific acceptance limits.

R2 · Abnormal conveyor sound−25 dBFS

If microphone level exceeds threshold, show a SILENT equipment warning and hold the line indicator. Calibrate to normal machine sound.
